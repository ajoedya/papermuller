    Research topic

    Describe what you want to investigate. Use English search terms when possible.

    Add full-text PDFs (optional, before starting)

    Enter the article’s exact DOI. PDFs need a text layer (up to 12 MB and 150 pages). Files stay on the server; selected excerpts are sent to your AI provider in live mode.

      Review the plan

      Review the title, questions, assumptions, and scope. Remove any location or time period you did not request.

      Author-supplied manuscript sections (optional)

      Your Methods and Results are copied into the working manuscript as supplied, without verification. Empty sections remain marked as missing.
