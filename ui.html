    Paper language
    
    
    Choose the language for brainstorming, problem formulation, and the paper. The interface language stays separate.
    
    
    
      
    
        
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    Choose a listed language or enter another language or locale. Source quotations and author-supplied text are preserved. Changes apply to new content; existing drafts are not translated automatically. Model language support varies.
    
    
    Demo prose is scripted in English. Use live mode to generate prose in your chosen language, subject to model support.
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
    Before the research plan
    
    Explore ideas with AI
    
    
    Start with an interest, a practical problem, or the data you have. Discuss possible directions before choosing a research question.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
    
    
    
    Ideas are proposals, not verified research gaps. You will review the problem and scope before any literature search starts.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Research topic
    
    
    Describe what you want to investigate. English search terms usually find more papers.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Research settings
    
    
    These apply to the next run and are saved when you create a plan.
    
    
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    All extracted PDF text is sent to your model in page-labelled chunks. This may use more API calls. Image-only pages need OCR; inaccessible papers stay abstract-only unless full text is required.
    

    
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Full-text PDFs (optional)
    
    
    Add PDFs before starting. Enter the article's exact DOI. PDFs need a text layer (up to 12 MB and 150 pages). In live mode, all extracted PDF text is sent to your AI provider in page-labelled chunks.

      Check the title, questions, assumptions, and scope. Remove any location or time period you did not ask for.
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
      Author-supplied manuscript sections (optional)
      
      Your Methods and Results are copied as supplied, without translation or verification. Both sections are required for final release; drafts may leave them unfilled.

      Literature review draft
      
      
      IDs such as P1 refer to the source matrix. AI scores and matching excerpts do not replace reading the sources.
      
      
      
    
      Source matrix
      
      
      DOI and title are checked against Crossref. Matching text alone does not establish scientific support.
      
      
      
    
      Researcher review
      
      
      Tick each item after reading the sources. This records your review; it does not certify validation.
      
      
      
      
      
      
    
      Full-text access & reading
      
      
      Full text means extracted PDF text, not visual inspection of figures or scanned pages. The report shows what reached the model; it does not certify comprehension.
      
      
      
    
      
    
      Editorial checks
      
      
      Local suggestions with source line numbers. Quotes, numbers, citations, and author text are never rewritten. This is not an AI detector or a scientific review.
